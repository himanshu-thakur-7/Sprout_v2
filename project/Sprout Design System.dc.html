Design system, components & Pip
Playful like Duolingo, calmer and warmer. The signature is the hard bottom edge: every card and button sits on a solid 4px ledge in its darker shade, with no blur, so it reads as a physical, pressable thing. Proud numbers are huge. Everything else is quiet.

08 · PIP
Moods
A soft jellybean seed. Moods come only from swapping or transforming parts: eyes, mouth, leaf rotation, arm pose and body offset. The character is never redrawn. The leaves always stay on top of the head, and the arms stay short and stubby.
